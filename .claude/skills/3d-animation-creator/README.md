# 3D Animation Creator

Jack Roberts' scroll-driven video site builder, from his Drive folder
(2026-03-27), installed 2026-09-24. Give it a short video with a white first
frame and it builds an Apple-style scroll-scrub page. Needs ffmpeg. Its
default look (Space Grotesk, glass cards, starscape) is the skill's, not
the lab's: on kellylucas.dev, CLAUDE.md's visual rules win. A newer
"3D-Website-Skill.zip" (2026-08-06) sits in Jack's Drive and may supersede
this one.
