After hours

On the side I build small software for exactly one customer: me. Magpie is the web clipper I use every day. The design desk is my public swipe file. Clue d’État (opens in a new tab) is game-night trivia hosted by Napoleon, hat and all. The Pretzel Protocol is an atlas for a family trip to Berlin, Munich and Prague.

None of it is for sale. The rule is real over cute: if it doesn’t work, it doesn’t go on the shelf.

Places, drawn

I collect places. The drawings on the homepage are spots I love, in a fine navy pen style called Heliopolis, which started as the team space I designed for HelioFlux. They’re made with an image model in my style, and the colophon says so.

Why a painting

This page began as the film at the top: three small cars, one lake, a driver who gets a close-up, and ducks who get their water back. It was painted in code with Claude, one “more watercolor, please” at a time.

It didn’t match the poster on the homepage one bit, so it got a page of its own. The sky, the sun, the mountains and the lake below were painted with the same brushes.
