        Save from anywhere

        Three ways in. Every one lands in this library, and Magpie files it.

        Chrome on your Mac

        The feather is the small Magpie button in Chrome. Press it on any page, or press Alt+Shift+M, and the page is saved.

        1. In Chrome on the Mac, open chrome://extensions and press the reload arrow on Magpie Web Clipper.
        2. Open this page there. A “Connect this Chrome” button shows up right here. Press it and you’re done.
        Paste the key by hand instead

        Open the feather, choose More, then Connect, and paste this key. Leave the address as kellylucas.dev.

        Treat it like a password: it can add to and read your library. It changes if the Magpie secret in Vercel is ever changed.

        iPhone and iPad

        The quick way: copy a link, open Magpie, press Add a link. To save straight from the Share button, make a shortcut once:

        1. In the Shortcuts app, press +. Add the action URL Encode and set it to Shortcut Input.
        2. Add the action Open URLs. Paste this address, then add URL Encoded Text right after it:
        3. Name it “Save to Magpie”, press the round “i”, and turn on Show in Share Sheet.

        From then on: Share, then “Save to Magpie”. Add this page to your Home Screen too and Magpie opens like an app.

        Any other computer

        Drag this to the bookmarks bar and press it on any page: Save to Magpie

        One library: the feather, the phone and the Mac all save here. Private, and never indexed.
