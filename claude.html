Plant a thought.

Give it a word or let the garden choose. Meaning selects the whole organism: roots, branching, posture, leaf shape, growth rhythm, color, and what it does after dark. Some flower; others become strange foliage, pods, or spores. The same seed always remembers its DNA across 39 forms and 22 color climates. If a meaning is not recognized, the garden says so and grows a distinctly letter-shaped Mystery Seedling instead of inventing a connection. Add three beside the five lab plants, or clear the plot to open eight visitor-only spaces. Rain creates lasting growth in every root, stem, and leaf.
