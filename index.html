Magpie

In use since February 2026Built for one

My web clipper and private library. It’s the tool I open most, and it files everything I find into two nests at once.

Magpie’s full record
  • ClipPress the feather, or Alt Shift M, on any page. Add tags and a note.
  • Two nests at onceA full Markdown note lands in my Obsidian vault, and a card lands in a searchable gallery. Clip it again and it updates instead of duplicating.
  • Transcripts and papersYouTube clips bring their transcripts. Papers bring the open-access PDF, and it never goes around a paywall.
  • Bulk workbenchTakes a pasted pile or a stack of screenshots, read with OCR on my own Mac.
  • Galaxy mapClusters everything I’ve clipped. Design clips also sort themselves onto the public design desk.
