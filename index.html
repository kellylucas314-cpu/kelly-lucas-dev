Magpie, my web clipper and private library.

It’s the tool I open most, and it files everything I find into two nests at once.

In use since February 2026Built for one

  • Clip

    Press the feather, or Alt Shift M, on any page. Add tags and a note.

  • Two nests at once

    A full Markdown note lands in my Obsidian vault, and a card lands in a searchable gallery. Clip it again and it updates instead of duplicating.

  • Find it again

    A searchable gallery and a galaxy map of everything I’ve clipped. Design clips sort themselves onto the public design desk.

279

clips from 258 sites. 31 carry my own notes, 10 are papers and 8 are videos with transcripts, filed under 257 tags. Counted Aug 19, 2026.

Private

The library sits behind a password. You can’t install Magpie; it’s built for exactly one person.
