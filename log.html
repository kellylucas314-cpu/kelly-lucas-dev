The log

What shipped, what broke, and what got learned, newest first. Building in public means keeping the receipts.

  1. Oct 5, 2026

    v1.0 The lab grew up. Real projects up front, Magpie first, and the toys moved to the playground. New drawings of places I love, in my Heliopolis style and this site’s own six colors. Lato for everything, the same face Heliopolis uses.

  2. Sep 24, 2026

    v0.9.1 The design desk got five piles. The HelioFlux website (with the house style PDF and a 25-file style pack), Heliopolis, Pretzel Protocol, resources and examples. The Magpie feather now guesses the pile before you clip.

  3. Sep 24, 2026

    v0.9 The design desk opened. A swipe file for UI with galleries, sites, videos, and three notebook pages. The Magpie clipper got a second job feeding it.

  4. Jun 10, 2026

    midnight The lab built its own random number generator. Out of camera shot noise. The universe splitter now spends homemade photons.

  5. Jun 10, 2026

    night The pet got a gene lab, and a quantum wing opened. A double slit you can fire one particle at a time, a cat in a box, spooky action, and a universe splitter. Spark notes grew to 28 voices. The cat is fine.

  6. Jun 10, 2026

    later A pet, a panel, a noise room. And a field guide to keep track of all the rooms. Claude was left unsupervised for one afternoon.

  7. Jun 10, 2026

    v0.7 The site became a lab. GSAP moved in: draggable stickers, confetti when you ship, a konami code. Claude planted a pixel garden.

  8. May 11, 2026

    v0.6 Spark notes shipped. A living quote wall with favorites that survive a refresh, thanks to localStorage.

  9. May 7, 2026

    v0.5 kellylucas.dev went live. First domain, first deploy, real internet.

  10. May 7, 2026
