The playground

Side rooms I built for fun while I was learning the web. None of them are useful, and all of them work. They keep the old lab look on purpose, since this is where the site started.

  • Pixel gardenA tiny generative garden Claude planted. Plant a thought and see what grows.
  • The petSmall, round, lives in your browser, judges nothing. It has a gene lab now.
  • The panelSwitches and dials, and one button you should not press.
  • The noise roomFive notes, eight steps, zero wrong answers.
  • The quantum wingA double slit you fire one particle at a time, a cat in a box, and a universe splitter.
  • The entropy tapHomemade quantum randomness, straight from your camera’s photons.
  • Spark notesA living quote wall. Borrowed courage and saved lines.
  • The audition room97 fonts walked in. Heliora walked out, and later handed the job to Lato.
  • Nothing here A missing doorLeads nowhere. On purpose.
