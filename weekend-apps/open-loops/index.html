What is real and what is fixture
  • Real: capture, clarify, edit, move, complete, undo, search, filters, project grouping, and local persistence in this browser.
  • Fixture: all projects, tasks, people and links, including everything in Agent work and Needs Kelly. Agent snapshots are static sample text with a fixed snapshot time.
  • Absent on purpose: due dates, reminders, streaks, scores, an all-tasks wall, and any connection to another service. This sample demo has no sync or account connection.
