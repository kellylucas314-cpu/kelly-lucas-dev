A team scheduling prototype, shown with sample data.

Northwind Labs is an invented company with five invented people. The prototype books time with them: pick a person, pick a meeting type, pick a time in your own timezone, leave your details, confirm.

Sample previewMock calendar

  • Who

    Five fictional teammates, each with their own working hours and timezone: New York, Berlin, Chicago on an overnight shift, Sydney and Kolkata.

  • Meetings

    Each person offers their own types and lengths, from a 15 minute intro call to a 1 hour technical deep dive.

  • Steps

    Person, meeting, time, details, confirm, with the booking so far kept beside the form.

  • Afterwards

    An organizer view, and a link at the end of a booking for rescheduling or cancelling.

Mock calendar only

Availability comes from local fixture files. Nothing is connected to Google Calendar or any other real calendar, and no email, invitation or notification is ever sent. Every name, meeting and time slot in it is made up.
